Applied Economics • Tsinghua University

Chen (Charlie) Fang

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Economics at Tsinghua University, advised by Jing Cao and co-supervised by Mun Ho (Harvard).

My research focuses on the spatial economic effects of energy investments, climate change, and forced displacement. Alongside my academic work, I serve as a Statistics Assistant (formerly an Economist Intern) in UNHCR Jordan's Development Unit under Marguerite Duponchel's supervision. My policy-relevant work examines Syrian refugee return, welfare, and mental health.

I am visiting the Harvard China Project during the 2026-2027 academic year.

Primary fields: Environmental and Energy Economics; Spatial Economics

Secondary fields: Climate Change; International Development; Forced Displacement

Featured Research

What Is a Transmission Line Worth?

General-Equilibrium Returns to China's Ultra-High-Voltage Direct-Current Lines

with Mun Ho and Jing Cao

Using a general-equilibrium framework, this paper evaluates how large-scale electricity-transmission investment reshapes regional resource allocation, industrial activity, and welfare in China.
