Applied Economics • Tsinghua University

Chen (Charlie) Fang

Welcome to my webpage! I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Economics at Tsinghua University, advised by Jing Cao and co-supervised by Mun Ho (Harvard).

My research interests include environmental and energy economics, climate change, and international development. I also work with UNHCR Jordan as a Statistics Assistant (individual contractor), with recent work on refugee return, displacement-related welfare, and policy-relevant data analysis. I hope to work for an international organization to conduct valuable research.
