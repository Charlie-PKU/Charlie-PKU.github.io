Applied Economics • Tsinghua University

Chen (Charlie) Fang

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Economics at Tsinghua University, advised by Jing Cao and co-supervised by Mun Ho (Harvard).

My research focuses on the spatial economic effects of energy investments, climate change, and forced displacement. Alongside my academic work, I serve as a Statistics Assistant (individual contractor) with UNHCR Jordan, contributing to policy-relevant analysis of refugee return, welfare, and population dynamics. My long-term goal is to work as an economist at an international organization, such as the World Bank or the United Nations.

I am visiting the Harvard China Project during the 2026-2027 academic year.
