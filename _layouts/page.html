Applied Economics • Tsinghua University

Chen Fang

Welcome to my webpage! I am a Ph.D. student in Applied Economics at Tsinghua University, advised by Jing Cao and co-supervised by Mun Ho.

My research focuses on climate change economics, development, and quantitative welfare analysis. I also work with UNHCR Jordan as a Statistics Assistant (individual contractor), with recent work on refugee return, displacement-related welfare, and policy-relevant data analysis.
